## 📊 Project Overview

The **McDonald's Sales Analysis Dashboard** is a Business Intelligence and Data Analytics project developed using Microsoft Excel. It provides an interactive and visual representation of sales performance, profitability, customer growth, geographical sales distribution, and customer satisfaction.

The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful insights through charts, visualizations, KPI indicators, and comparative analysis, helping businesses monitor performance and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze monthly sales performance for 2021 and 2022.
- Monitor total sales, profit, and customer KPIs.
- Compare actual business performance against predefined targets.
- Analyze country-wise sales contributions.
- Evaluate customer satisfaction across multiple service dimensions.
- Identify business growth opportunities and performance gaps.
- Support strategic decision-making through data visualization.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
- Monthly sales comparison between 2021 and 2022.
- Identification of sales trends and patterns.
- Analysis of annual sales growth.
- Identification of highest and lowest performing months.

### 2. KPI Performance Monitoring
Tracks three major business performance indicators:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Number of Customers

Compares actual performance with predefined business targets.

### 3. Country-wise Sales Analysis
- Visualizes sales distribution across seven countries.
- Identifies high-performing and low-performing markets.
- Compares geographical sales contributions.

### 4. Customer Satisfaction Analysis

Evaluates customer satisfaction across five dimensions:

- Speed
- Quality
- Hygiene
- Service
- Availability

Helps identify areas requiring improvements in customer experience.

### 5. Business Intelligence and Visualization
- Interactive dashboard presentation.
- Graphical representation of business metrics.
- KPI-based performance monitoring.
- Comparative charts and visual insights.
